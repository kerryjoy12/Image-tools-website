Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 7, 2026

PixelTools is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and what rights you have over it. The most important thing to know is this: PixelTools does not collect, store, or upload your images.

1. Images Are Processed in Your Browser

Every PixelTools tool — the compressor, resizer, converter, and cropper — runs 100% in your browser using the Canvas API and the FileReader API. When you upload a photo, it is read into your device's memory as a data URL. It is processed, resized, converted, and cropped there. It is never sent to any server, stored on a database, or shared with any third party. The original image is deleted from memory as soon as the tool finishes producing your output file.

This design means PixelTools has no access to your photos, and therefore no obligation to keep them. It also means PixelTools cannot use your images for advertising, training, or any other purpose.

2. What Information We Do Collect

PixelTools collects only the information that the browser itself makes available to a website, and nothing more than is necessary to operate the tools. The data we could potentially see includes:

  • Your IP address — visible to every website you visit, and used only to deliver your files and run the tools.
  • Your browser type and operating system — used to optimise the interface for your device.
  • Pages you visit — used to improve our service and to generate anonymous statistics.
  • Files you upload — processed entirely in memory and never stored.

We do not collect, store, or transmit your images. We do not create user accounts. We do not send newsletters. We do not sell your data.

3. Cookies and Similar Technologies

We use cookies and equivalent local storage to remember your cookie consent choice so that the consent banner does not appear on every visit. We also use them to remember your theme preference (light or dark mode). These cookies do not contain any personal or identifying information, and they expire when you close your browser session.

We do not use cookies to track you across websites. We do not use third-party tracking scripts. All cookies are set by the PixelTools domain itself.

4. Third-Party Services and Advertising

PixelTools uses Google AdSense as an advertising service, but only after you give consent, and only in the European Economic Area and United Kingdom. Ads are served from Google's servers, and Google may use cookies and similar technologies to serve advertisements and to compile anonymous statistics about how you interact with our site. You can learn about how Google uses data through the Google Privacy & Terms and Google's Ads Settings pages.

Our site also uses third-party libraries to provide functionality, including Cropper.js for the cropper tool and JSZip for multi-file downloads. These libraries are loaded from cdnjs.cloudflare.com and are governed by their own terms of service. They do not collect your personal data.

5. GDPR Rights (For European Users)

If you are resident in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom, you have the following rights under the General Data Protection Regulation:

  • Right of access: You can request a copy of any personal data we hold about you.
  • Right to rectification: You can ask us to correct any inaccurate data.
  • Right to erasure: You can ask us to delete your data, subject to any legal obligations we must comply with.
  • Right to restriction: You can ask us to limit how we use your data in certain circumstances.
  • Right to object: You can object to our use of your data for direct marketing.
  • Right to data portability: You can request your data in a structured, machine-readable format.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at the email address below. We will respond within 30 days.

6. CCPA Rights (For California Users)

If you are a resident of California, you have the following rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act:

  • Right to know: You can ask what personal information we collect.
  • Right to delete: You can request deletion of your personal information.
  • Right to opt out: You can opt out of the sale of your personal information. We do not sell personal information.
  • Right to non-discrimination: We will not discriminate against you for exercising your rights.

To exercise these rights, contact us at the email address below.

7. Children's Privacy

PixelTools is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect information from children under the age of 13. If you are a child, please do not use our tools with personal photographs, and ask a parent or guardian for help.

8. Security

Because we do not store your images, the primary security consideration is the security of the communication channel between your browser and our server. We use HTTPS to encrypt the connection, which protects your information in transit. The tools are open-source, and the client-side code is public and auditable.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Any changes will be published on this page with a new date. We encourage you to review this page periodically so that you understand how PixelTools protects your privacy.
