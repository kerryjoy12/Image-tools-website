Cookie Policy

Last updated: November 7, 2026

This Cookie Policy explains how PixelTools uses cookies and similar technologies on our website, and how you can control them.

What Is a Cookie?

A cookie is a small text file that a website stores in your browser. Cookies allow the website to remember your preferences, keep you signed in, and remember your choices from one visit to the next. PixelTools uses cookies in two situations: to serve advertisements and to remember your consent.

Cookies We Use

PixelTools uses the following categories of cookies:

  • Strictly necessary cookies. These cookies are essential for the website to function. They remember your cookie consent choice so that the banner does not appear on every visit, and remember your light/dark theme preference. Without these cookies, the website would not work correctly.
  • Advertising cookies. When you have accepted advertising cookies, we and our partners may serve you personalised ads from Google AdSense. These cookies help us show you relevant ads and measure how they perform.
  • Third-party cookies. PixelTools loads third-party scripts for functionality, including Cropper.js for the cropper tool and JSZip for multi-file downloads. These scripts may use their own cookies. They are governed by the terms of the service provider.

How We Use Cookies

We use cookies for the following purposes:

  • To remember that you have accepted or rejected advertising cookies, so that you are not asked the same question every time you visit.
  • To remember your theme preference (light or dark mode) so that your choice persists between visits.
  • To help us understand how visitors use our site, so that we can improve the service. These statistics are anonymous and do not identify you.

Your Choices

You can control and manage cookies in several ways:

  • Cookie banner. When you visit the site, a cookie consent banner appears. You can accept all cookies, reject all cookies, or manage your preferences in the panel.
  • Browser settings. Most browsers allow you to control cookies through their settings. You can set your browser to block or delete cookies, or to warn you when a cookie is being set. You can find instructions for your browser in the browser's help section.
  • Third-party opt-out. You can opt out of personalised advertising from Google through the Google Ads Settings page at adssettings.google.com. You can also opt out of other third-party advertising providers through the Digital Advertising Alliance at aboutads.info.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Any changes will be published on this page with a new date. We encourage you to review this page periodically so that you understand how PixelTools uses cookies.
