About PixelTools

PixelTools is a free, privacy-first service that gives you professional image tools in your browser — with no signup and no server uploads.

Why We Built PixelTools

We built PixelTools because image editing online so often comes with hidden costs. Most "free" tools upload your photos to a server, force you to create an account, or quietly use your images for advertising. We wanted to build something different: a set of tools that treat your photos as if they were our own.

Our Mission: Privacy First, Always

Every PixelTools tool is built on one simple principle: your images never leave your device. The tools use the browser's Canvas API and FileReader API to process images entirely in memory. No image is stored in a database, sent to a server, or used for anything other than producing your output file. This is the foundation of everything we do.

What We Offer

PixelTools provides five free online image tools:

  • Image Compressor — shrink JPG, PNG, and WebP files with a live quality slider.
  • Image Resizer — resize by pixels or percentage, with aspect-ratio lock and social presets.
  • JPG to PNG Converter — convert between JPG and PNG, with transparency preserved.
  • WebP Converter — convert to and from WebP with a quality control slider.
  • Image Cropper — drag-to-crop with fixed ratios, rotation, and flip.

Every tool is free forever. There are no trials, no upgrades, no watermarks, and no paywalls.

How We Earn Revenue

PixelTools's tools are free, so we need a sustainable way to keep the lights on. We serve personalised advertisements through Google AdSense in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom, but only after you have given your consent. Our advertising is clearly labelled, never interferes with your work, and never covers or overlaps any of the tool buttons or the download button.

Our Values

  • Privacy: Your images are yours alone. No uploads, no account, no data collection.
  • Openness: We are building an open platform where every tool is transparent about what it does.
  • Quality: Easy to use, fast, and built to last. Every tool is tested across devices.
  • Free: We will never charge for a tool, and we will never ask for your credit card.
