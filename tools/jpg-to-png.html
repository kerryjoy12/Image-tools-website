JPG to PNG Converter

100% Client-Side Convert JPG to PNG for lossless quality and full transparency. Also converts PNG to JPG. No files leave your device.

PixelTools JPG to PNG Converter is the fastest, safest way to change the format of a photo, screenshot, or graphics file. The JPG and PNG formats solve different problems. JPG is compressed and loses detail over time, which is great for photographs. PNG is lossless, which means every byte of the original is preserved, and it supports full alpha transparency, so the parts of the image that should be invisible are actually transparent.

Use this tool when you need a JPG file to behave like a PNG. Maybe you took a photo on your phone, and now you need to place it on a coloured or patterned website background — PNG is the right answer because the transparency channel means the background colour shows through. Maybe you received a JPG where a logo needs to sit on top of a gradient, or you are archiving images and want lossless storage. The converter re-encodes the pixels into a new container without touching a single pixel's colour value.

How to Convert a JPG to PNG

  1. Upload. Drag & drop a JPG onto the upload zone, click to browse, or paste a JPG from your clipboard. You can also do the reverse — PNG to JPG — using the toggle in the upper-left corner.
  2. Pick an output format. Choose PNG for lossless quality and transparency, or JPG for a smaller file. The default is PNG as requested by the page title, but the tool handles both directions.
  3. Process. The conversion happens instantly. There is no queue, no waiting, and no colour shift.
  4. Download. Save the converted file. The original is never overwritten and is deleted from memory after the output is produced.

When to Use PNG Over JPG

PNG is the right choice whenever you need text, sharp lines, or a transparent background to stay perfectly crisp. Logos, icons, screenshots of code or documentation, and graphics with flat colour blocks are all better preserved as PNG. JPG tends to soften edges and introduce artefacts around text, which makes it a poor format for any image that contains fine detail.

When to Use JPG Over PNG

If your PNG has a photographic background or contains millions of colours, it can balloon to several megabytes. JPG compresses those photos far more efficiently. The converter lets you go back from PNG to JPG when you need a smaller file for email, web hosting, or a photo sharing platform.

Compatibility Notes

Every browser supports PNG. For JPG output, all modern browsers work with the standard baseline settings. Alpha channel PNGs are kept exactly as they are — the checker-board preview shows where transparency sits so you know the conversion is faithful.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does converting JPG to PNG increase the file size?

✓

Usually yes. PNG stores every pixel exactly, so a photographic JPG that has already lost detail will typically become a larger PNG. If you need a small file, convert to JPG instead, or if your PNG contains a photograph, use the WebP converter.

Does the conversion reduce image quality?

✓

No. The JPG-to-PNG conversion is lossless. Every pixel, colour, and channel from the JPG is copied into the PNG without any recompression of already-compressed data.

Can I convert PNG to JPG?

✓

Yes. Use the direction toggle above the upload zone. Note that transparent PNGs cannot be saved as JPG — the tool fills transparent areas with white and tells you when this happens, so you can decide whether that is acceptable.

What happens to the transparency of a PNG?

✓

Transparency is fully preserved. The preview visualises the alpha channel on a checkerboard background so you can confirm it is intact before you download.

Can I batch convert multiple JPGs?

✓

Yes. Upload several files at once and each is converted individually. Once the batch is done, you can download all results as a ZIP archive.

Before You Convert

  • Make a backup: Keep the original JPG or PNG until you are sure the converted file works for you.
  • Check for transparency: PNGs with transparency will stay transparent after conversion back to JPG, but the background will be filled white.
  • Watch file size: Lossless PNG files are bigger than JPG by design. Only use PNG when you need it.
